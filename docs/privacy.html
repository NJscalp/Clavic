Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Clavic ("Clavic", "we", "us") lets you create and edit videos and images from your own photos and text descriptions using artificial intelligence. We take your privacy seriously and only process the data needed to provide the app. This policy explains what we process, why, and your rights.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of the Clavic app is responsible for the processing described here. For any privacy request, contact us at Clavic.ai.app@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

  • Content you provide: the photos and videos you upload and the text prompts you enter to generate or edit content.
  • Generated content: the videos and images the AI creates for you.
  • Purchase data: when you buy credits or a subscription, the purchase is handled by Apple. We receive confirmation of the purchase but never your payment-card details.
  • Chat and analysis: your messages, attached reference files, relevant conversation history and generated images used in follow-up edits are processed when you request AI assistance.
  • Technical data: basic, non-identifying information needed to deliver requests reliably (e.g. error and connection status).

We use AppsFlyer for attribution and campaign measurement, including install, onboarding and purchase events. Advertising identifiers are subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Campaign and purchase events may be attributed to TikTok through AppsFlyer. We use RevenueCat to measure and manage subscription entitlements. These services receive technical or purchase-related data, not your photos or AI prompts. We do not collect your contacts or precise location.

Face data

Some photos you choose to upload may contain faces (for example a selfie you use in a fan-cam, image-edit or similar template). We treat this as face data and handle it as follows:

  • What we collect: only the photos you yourself select and upload as input. We do not scan your photo library, we do not use the camera in the background, and we do not create faceprints, biometric identifiers, face templates or any face-recognition data.
  • How we use it: solely to generate the specific image or video you requested (for example placing your face into a chosen template). We never use face data to identify or verify you, for advertising, for analytics, or to train AI models.
  • Sharing and permission: before any AI upload or request, Clavic explains what will be sent and asks for your permission. Your selected content is sent over HTTPS to our Railway-hosted backend and, depending on the tool, WaveSpeedAI, fal.ai, kie.ai or Anthropic for processing. Photos containing faces may be included. These services may use model and infrastructure sub-processors as described in their policies below. Clavic does not sell your face data.
  • Storage & retention: your finished results are stored locally on your device until you delete them. Processing providers may retain inputs, outputs and technical logs under their applicable retention and security policies. We do not promise a universal 24-hour deletion period. Contact us to request deletion from processors; deleting a local project does not automatically delete a provider's copy.

3. How and why we use your data

We use the photos, videos and prompts you provide solely to perform the generation or edit you requested. To do this, your input is sent over an encrypted connection to our processing backend and from there to the AI providers that run the models. Purchase and account data are used to unlock and restore your credits and subscription.

Your choice: tap Allow AI processing only if you agree to this sharing. You can choose Not now and browse the app without sending content to AI services. You can review or withdraw this permission in Settings → AI data sharing. Withdrawal stops new AI requests; it does not recall data already sent or cancel processing already underway. Reinstalling or resetting the app requires permission again.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6(1)): your consent (a) for sharing selected content with AI services, performance of our contract with you (b) for providing the service and purchases, and our legitimate interest (f) in operating and securing the app.

4. AI processing and providers (sub-processors)

Your content is processed by the following providers acting on our behalf:

  • Apple – in-app purchases and subscription transactions.
  • Railway – hosting of our processing backend: privacy policy.
  • WaveSpeedAI – image, video and language-model processing: privacy policy.
  • fal.ai – AI generation and processing: privacy policy.
  • kie.ai – AI generation and processing: privacy policy.
  • Anthropic – AI chat and analysis where routed to its API: commercial terms and data protection.
  • AppsFlyer – attribution and campaign measurement; RevenueCat – subscription and purchase events.

We limit AI sharing to the content needed for the requested feature. Our processing providers must protect shared personal data to the same or an equivalent standard as described in this policy, including confidentiality, restricted access, secure transmission and processing for the disclosed purposes. Their applicable data-processing terms and sub-processor safeguards govern processing on our behalf. Clavic does not use your photos or prompts to train its own models or for advertising. Provider-specific retention and model-processing terms apply; follow the links above for details. Processing may take place outside your country, including in the USA.

5. Storage and retention

Your finished videos and images are stored locally on your device. Uploaded input, generated output and security logs may also be held by processing providers according to the applicable service terms. Deleting a project in the app removes its local files. For requests concerning provider-held content, contact us using the details below.

6. Photo library access

Clavic only requests "add" access to your photo library, and only when you choose to save a result. We never read or scan your existing photos. Photos you pick as input are selected by you through Apple's system picker.

7. International transfers

Because some providers are located in the United States, your data may be transferred there. Such transfers are based on appropriate safeguards (e.g. the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including the GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting Clavic.ai.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

Clavic is not directed to children. You must be at least 17 years old, or the age of digital consent in your country, to use the app.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and limit access to data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the app and on this page. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email Clavic.ai.app@gmail.com.
